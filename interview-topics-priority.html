Interview Topics by Priority

One table per technology. Each topic is ranked by how many questions were asked on it. Data: 96 questions from Companies-Interview and 152 from Online Fetched Questions.

Priority 1 is the topic asked most often under the current filters. "Questions asked" counts every question that touches the topic (one question can count toward more than one topic). "Companies" is how many different companies asked it. Hover a company chip to preview its questions. Some Online Fetched reports list topics, not exact questions, and each topic counts once.
