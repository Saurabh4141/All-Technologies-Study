## Round 2 – Technical Interview (1 hour)

1. RAG application development: the procedure to build a notebook-based RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) application.
2. RAG application development: concepts like vector databases and the steps involved in RAG.
3. Coding challenge: find the longest repeated substring that appears at least twice in a string (for s = 'banana' the result is 'ana').
4. Coding challenge: detect a loop / cycle in a linked list.
5. LLM benchmarking: definitions of MMLU, HELM, HumanEval and Big-Bench.
6. Hyperparameters in LLMs: Top-K, Top-P, Temperature and similar.
7. Advanced concepts: CoT (Chain of Thought) reasoning.
8. Advanced concepts: Self-Attention, Encoder and Decoder, and their roles.
9. Advanced concepts: Generator and Discriminator used in GANs.
